About the Portal

The Smart Complaint and Grievance Portal provides a simple and efficient platform for students and staff to register complaints and follow their resolution online. It removes manual paperwork and repeated visits to administrative offices, and keeps the whole process transparent.

Traditional method

  • Paper forms and office visits
  • No idea who is handling the issue
  • No status updates
  • Repeated issues go unnoticed

With this portal

  • Submit online, by typing or by voice
  • Unique Complaint ID for every issue
  • Live status and admin remarks
  • Reports reveal recurring problems
