How a complaint is handled

The same four stages for every complaint, visible to you at every point.

  1. Submit

    Describe the issue by typing or by voice, pick the department and send it.

  2. Assign

    An administrator reviews it and assigns it to the responsible person.

  3. Act

    The status changes as work proceeds, with remarks explaining each update.

  4. Resolve and review

    Once closed, you can leave feedback on how it was handled.

For users

  • Register and log in securely
  • Submit a complaint by text or voice
  • Track the status and read remarks
  • Give feedback after resolution

For administrators

  • View all complaints by department
  • Assign complaints and update their status
  • Search and filter by category, status or date
  • Generate reports on complaints and resolution times
